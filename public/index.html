Attire & Color Etiquette

Auspicious Colors: Bright jewel tones, yellow for Haldi, emerald & teal for Sangeet, and pastel pinks/peaches for Anand Karaj.

Colors to Avoid: Plain black or stark white are traditionally associated with mourning in Eastern customs.

Head Coverings: Required at Gurdwara ceremonies. Beautiful ceremonial rumals and chunnis are provided at the entrance.

Gift Customs (Shagun)

The +1 Rule: Monetary envelopes (Shagun) typically end in '1' (e.g. ₹501, ₹1101, ₹2101, ₹5001) as an odd number signifies an unbroken cycle of prosperity.

Blessings: Elders bless the couple with touch of hands on head; feel free to exchange warm hugs and Namaste.

Dining & Dietary Respect

Strict Segregation: Separate gourmet kitchens for pure Jain (no root vegetables), Vegetarian, Halal, and Global cuisine.

Langar: During Sikh ceremonies, simple sanctified vegetarian food (Langar) is enjoyed together sitting as equals on the floor.
